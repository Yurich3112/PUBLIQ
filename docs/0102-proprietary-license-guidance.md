# Proprietary license guidance

## Classification and scope

The governing text is the root [LICENSE.md](../LICENSE.md), version 2.1, revised from
the supplied draft. It is a custom proprietary, restricted-access license with
confidentiality, contribution and owner-relicensing terms. It is not MIT,
Apache-2.0, an open-source license or a standard customer deployment license.
An enterprise writing style is not a legal certification.

The Owner is **Kacper Boś, trading as Kacper Boś IT**, a sole proprietor, using
**kacper.bos.it@gmail.com**. The business form and contact were supplied by the
Owner, not independently checked against a business register. No NIP, registered
address or separate corporate identity has been invented.

The license contains no fixed repository name or URL. It applies separately
where the Owner or an authorized representative intentionally adopts it for
Owner-controlled material. Copying it does not transfer rights in someone else's
repository. Third-party licenses retain their effect.

The repository currently contains documentation, not an implemented application.
This license does not establish rights in future code, external Contributions,
model weights or generated output that the Owner has not lawfully acquired.

## Material corrections to the supplied draft

| Topic | Revised treatment |
| --- | --- |
| Business identity | Names the natural person operating the sole proprietorship, rather than treating the business name as a separate legal entity |
| Contract formation | Requires prior availability and legally effective recorded assent, technical access alone is not conclusive acceptance |
| Contribution ownership | Separates a limited evaluation license from a formally executed transfer or exclusive license |
| Exploitation rights | Requires identification of particular Contributions and express known fields, excludes blanket acquisition of unspecified future works |
| Moral rights | Preserves non-transferable rights, specific lawful consents are documented separately |
| Contributor credit | Allows control of optional publicity while preserving truthful authorship and mandatory notices |
| Confidentiality | Adds public-information, prior-knowledge, independent-development and lawful-disclosure exceptions |
| Third-party dependencies | Preserves independent license rights and required notices, recorded approval can cover permitted dependencies |
| External AI and services | Requires permission for non-public uploads, does not pretend to establish ownership of AI output or unavailable training provenance |
| Amendments and relicensing | Applies changes prospectively with required acceptance and form, preserves third-party rights and prior irrevocable grants |
| Liability | Preserves the USD 10,000 cap for liability capable of limitation, removes the contradictory exclusion of every direct loss and adds mandatory carve-outs |
| Indemnity | Narrows liability to attributable breaches or rights failures and controlled third-party claims |
| Remedies and venue | Preserves court discretion, evidence requirements, written-form requirements and mandatory jurisdiction |
| Power of attorney | Removes automatic representation authority purportedly arising from repository access |

## Statutory checks and their limits

The following checks use official published consolidated texts. They address
selected drafting issues, not every amendment, conflict-of-laws question or
possible contractual relationship. Review the execution copy for the actual
parties and use case before relying on it.

- The [Copyright Act, Articles 16, 41, 50, 53, 67 and 74-77](https://api.sejm.gov.pl/eli/acts/DU/2025/24/text.pdf)
  addresses moral rights, identified exploitation fields, future-work limits,
  written transfers, exclusive licenses and software-specific exceptions. The
  revised text does not treat an unsigned commit as a completed assignment.
- The [Civil Code, Articles 43(4), 78, 78(1), 384-385(5) and 473](https://api.sejm.gov.pl/eli/acts/DU/2026/795/text.pdf)
  informs the proprietor identity, signature formalities, prior provision of
  terms, protected-party safeguards and limits on excluding intentional harm.
  Ordinary email permission is distinguished from a qualified electronic signature.
- The [Code of Civil Procedure, Article 46](https://api.sejm.gov.pl/eli/acts/DU/2026/468/text.pdf)
  addresses written venue agreements and preserves exclusive jurisdiction.
  An Opole clause is therefore conditional on the required form and applicable
  jurisdiction rules.

There is no claim that an English repository file alone secures acceptance,
assigns all contributor rights or guarantees an enforceable injunction. Signed
instruments and the applicable facts remain essential. A Polish lawyer should
review contributor transfers, compensation, liability and international or
protected-party use before the text is used operationally.

## Adoption and contribution workflow

1. Verify the Owner's full registered business particulars. Provide the NIP and
   service address in private execution copies where required. Do not invent
   missing information or publish completed signature records unnecessarily.
2. Provide the applicable license version before contractual access and retain
   evidence of its delivery, acceptance, scope and any separate permissions.
3. Identify the authorized review, development, test or deployment purpose.
   Repository access is not a general license to customer deployments.
4. Before production incorporation, execute the
   [contribution assignment template](0103-contribution-assignment-template.md)
   or a suitable employment or contractor instrument. Identify existing works
   and secure the actual rights holder's signature and authority.
5. Preserve the signed agreement, attached license version, work identifiers,
   compensation selection and approved Background Materials securely. Later
   unidentified work requires a further properly executed schedule.
6. Maintain the [third-party register](0101-third-party-licenses.md), resolved
   dependency inventory and required notices. A proprietary label does not
   authorize removing licenses from libraries, runtimes or images.
7. Before public release or relicensing, verify the complete chain of rights.
   Designate the exact released materials and preserve prior grants that cannot
   be revoked. Publication does not automatically release other private material.

The assignment template is intentionally incomplete until the parties complete
and sign it. These documentation files are not evidence that any external
Contributor has accepted terms or transferred rights.
